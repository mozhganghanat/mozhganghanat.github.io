About Me

I am an Educational Technology researcher with an academic and professional background in educational technology, inclusive education, and special education.

I hold an M.Sc. in Educational Technology from Allameh Tabataba'i University, Tehran, where I graduated ranked first in my cohort. My research and professional experiences include educational technology, inclusive learning, assistive technologies, artificial intelligence in education, and technology-supported learning for students with special needs.

I also have several years of experience working with elementary school students and supporting learners with diverse educational needs.
