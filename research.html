Research Perspective

Why this area matters to me

I am interested in exploring how educational technologies can contribute to more inclusive and accessible learning environments for learners who may face barriers to participation, communication, or educational opportunities.

My interest in this area is grounded in a strong belief in equity, fairness, and the right of every learner to meaningful educational opportunities.

I believe that technology should not simply make learning more efficient. It should also help reduce barriers and create opportunities for children and learners who have historically been underserved by conventional educational systems.

Research Direction

Creating more equitable learning opportunities

My broader research direction focuses on understanding how technology can be designed and used in ways that respect individual differences, support participation, and promote more equitable educational experiences.

Every learner deserves the opportunity to learn, participate, and thrive.

This principle guides my interest in educational technology and my commitment to more inclusive and equitable learning environments.
