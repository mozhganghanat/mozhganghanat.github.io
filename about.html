About Me

Researcher, Teacher &
Advocate for Inclusive Learning

I am a researcher and teacher interested in how education and technology can create more inclusive, accessible, and equitable learning opportunities for children.

My perspective

Education should make room for every learner.

I am a researcher and teacher with several years of experience working with children in educational settings. I am currently continuing my work as a teacher while pursuing my research interests in educational technology and inclusive education.

My main interest is in understanding how education and technology can create more inclusive, accessible, and equitable learning opportunities for children with diverse educational needs.

Through both my teaching experience and research, I have become particularly interested in the challenges faced by children who may need additional educational support. I believe that every child should have the opportunity to learn, participate, communicate, and develop their abilities in an environment that respects their individual needs.

My work therefore sits at the intersection of education, technology, inclusion, and equity, with a particular interest in using technology to support children who are often underserved by conventional educational approaches.

My Goal

My goal is simple.

To explore how education and technology can help make learning more inclusive, accessible, and equitable for every child.
